Our Local Radius

Every producer must be located within {{ radius_limit }} miles of Bristol city centre. This ensures that everything on the platform is genuinely local - grown, baked or made right here in our community.

How We Calculate Food Miles

Food miles are calculated for every item in every order, based on the distance between the producer’s registered location and the delivery location. These values are aggregated into each order and displayed transparently so customers can make informed, sustainable choices.

Freshness & Waste Reduction

We enforce strict freshness rules to protect customers and reduce waste. Products too close to their expiry date are automatically removed from sale, and items approaching expiry receive automatic reductions to help them sell in time.

Supporting Local Producers

We charge a simple, transparent 5% commission per order per producer. There are no listing fees or hidden charges. Producers keep control of their pricing, and our platform helps them reach local customers without the overhead of traditional retail.

Why It Matters

By choosing local food, you're supporting independent producers, reducing carbon emissions, and helping build a resilient, sustainable food system for Bristol.
