Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how we use cookies and similar technologies on our website. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how data is used.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site function properly, improve performance, and provide insights into how visitors use the site.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the website to function. They enable core features such as:

  • Logging in and maintaining your session
  • CSRF protection for secure forms
  • Shopping cart functionality

These cookies do not require consent under UK GDPR and PECR.

Functional Cookies

These cookies remember your preferences, such as:

  • Cookie consent settings
  • UI or display preferences

These cookies require your consent.

Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors use the website so we can improve performance. Examples include:

  • Google Analytics
  • Hotjar
  • Matomo

These cookies require explicit opt‑in consent.

3. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to:

  • Ensure the website works correctly
  • Improve user experience
  • Analyse site performance (only if you consent)

4. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You can change your cookie settings at any time using the cookie preferences popup. You can choose:

  • Accept all cookies
  • Reject non‑essential cookies
  • Customise your preferences

5. Third‑Party Cookies

Some cookies may be set by third‑party services such as analytics tools or embedded content. These are only activated if you give consent.

6. How Long Cookies Are Stored

Cookie duration varies:

  • Session cookies: deleted when you close your browser
  • Persistent cookies: stored for a set period (e.g., 30–365 days)
